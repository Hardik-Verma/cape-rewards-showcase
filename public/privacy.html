Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary for the functionality of our platform. This includes:

  • Anonymous session IDs generated locally on your browser.
  • IP addresses temporarily logged for security and anti-bypass rate-limiting.
  • Discord IDs only when you choose to redeem a key via our official Discord bot.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your session ID is used exclusively to verify that you have successfully completed a sponsored task on the third-party CPA network and to securely generate your redemption key. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with marketing agencies.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use local storage within your browser (`localStorage`) to save your session ID and completed survey keys. This ensures you do not lose your reward if you accidentally close the tab. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

4. Security

The security of your data is paramount. We employ advanced anti-bypass mechanisms and rate-limiting to protect our servers and your session integrity. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
